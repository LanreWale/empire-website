The Commercial License, Explained

Royalty-Free Does Not Mean Public Domain

Royalty-free is a licensing model — it means you pay once (or in this case, get them free) and can use the image multiple times without additional fees. It does not mean the images are in the public domain or that you can claim ownership. The license in this bundle grants you commercial and personal use rights without attribution.

What You Can Do

  • Use photos in blog posts, social media ads, website designs, and email headers
  • Use in presentations and printed materials
  • Use in product packaging and client projects
  • Crop, resize, add text overlays, and modify the images
  • Use in digital downloads and lead magnets

What You Cannot Do

  • Resell the photos as standalone files
  • Redistribute them in a competing stock photo bundle
  • Claim you photographed them
  • Use them in contexts that could be considered defamatory or offensive

These restrictions are standard for royalty-free licensing and are documented in the included license PDF.
