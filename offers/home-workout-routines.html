Three Levels — One Complete Program

Every fitness level gets its own complete 30-day plan with exercises calibrated for difficulty, volume, and intensity. Choose your starting level and follow the calendar — no guesswork, no random workouts.

Beginner Level

15–25 minutes per day, 5 workout days per week. Focus is on learning proper form, breathing technique, and building base strength and endurance. Perfect for complete beginners or people returning to exercise after a long break.

Intermediate Level

25–35 minutes per day, 5 workout days per week. Introduces compound movements, supersets, and circuits. Best for people with 3–6 months of consistent training experience.

Advanced Level

35–45 minutes per day, 6 workout days per week. High-intensity circuits, plyometrics, and complex movement patterns. For experienced lifters and athletes looking to maintain or improve performance without a gym.

Five Workout Types For Balanced Fitness

Full-Body Strength

Push-ups, squats, lunges, planks, and their progressions. Builds muscle and bone density across every major muscle group.

HIIT Cardio

Interval-based circuits alternating 30 seconds of work with 15 seconds of rest. Burns calories during and after the workout through the "afterburn effect."

Flexibility & Mobility

Dynamic stretching, yoga-inspired flows, and mobility drills. Improves range of motion, reduces stiffness, and prevents injury.
