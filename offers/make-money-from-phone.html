The Task Priority Framework

Not every earning activity is worth your time. Some pay $2/hour; others pay $25/hour. The task priority framework helps you identify the difference.

Calculate Your Effective Hourly Rate

Effective hourly rate = Money earned ÷ Time spent

Example: A 15-minute survey that pays $2.50 = $10/hour effective rate.

A 30-minute survey that pays $1.50 = $3/hour effective rate.

Task A: 15 min × $2.50 payout = $10.00/hr
Task B: 30 min × $1.50 payout = $3.00/hr
Winner: Task A (by 3.3x)

How to Apply It

For your first week, track every task you complete and the time it took. At the end of the week, calculate the effective hourly rate for each app. Most people discover that 2–3 apps are doing all the heavy lifting while 5–10 are wasting their time.

The Three-Tier System

After one week of tracking, sort your apps into three tiers:

  • Tier 1 — $15+/hour: Focus most of your time here. These are your moneymakers.
  • Tier 2 — $8–$15/hour: Good enough for dead time. Use during commutes and waiting rooms.
  • Tier 3 — Under $8/hour: Cut them. No matter how easy they are, they're not worth the time.

Review your tiers monthly. Apps change their payout rates, and new apps appear. The tracker spreadsheet makes this a 10-minute monthly task.

Pro Tips

  • Gift cards often pay more. Many apps offer a 5–10% bonus for choosing gift cards over PayPal.
  • Batch your cash-outs. Wait until you hit $25–$50 before cashing out to reduce transaction frequency and fees.
  • Track every payment. Use the included tracker to log every payout. Important for taxes if you earn over $600 from any single app.
  • Watch the minimums. Some apps set minimums of $25+, which can mean months of accumulation before you can withdraw.
