The 8 Core Exercises (With Form Cues)

Every workout in the 30-day plan is built from these eight fundamental bodyweight exercises. Master the form before adding reps.

1. Bodyweight Squat

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Lower your hips as if sitting in a chair. Keep your chest up and knees tracking over your toes.

Form cue: Go as low as you can without your lower back rounding. If your heels lift, widen your stance.

2. Knee Push-Up (Modified)

Hands under shoulders, knees on the floor. Lower your chest until your elbows reach 90 degrees. Push back up.

Form cue: Keep your body in a straight line from knees to head. Don't let your hips sag or rise.

3. Walking Lunge

Step forward with one leg, lowering your back knee until it nearly touches the ground. Push through your front heel to stand, then step forward with the other leg.

Form cue: Keep your torso upright. If you can't balance, do reverse lunges instead (step backward).

4. Plank Hold

Forearms on the ground, elbows under shoulders. Body in a straight line from head to heels. Hold.

Form cue: Squeeze your glutes and abs. If your hips sag, drop to your knees â€” but keep your body straight.

5. Glute Bridge

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor. Push your hips toward the ceiling until your body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders.

Form cue: Squeeze your glutes at the top. Don't arch your lower back â€” the movement comes from your hips.

6. Bird Dog

On hands and knees, extend your right arm forward and left leg back simultaneously. Hold for 2 seconds, return, switch sides.

Form cue: Keep your hips level. Don't rotate your torso â€” the movement is straight forward and back.

7. Dead Bug

Lie on your back, arms up, knees bent at 90 degrees. Lower your right arm and left leg toward the ground simultaneously, then return and switch.

Form cue: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Slow and controlled beats fast and sloppy.

8. Standing Calf Raise

Stand with feet hip-width apart. Rise onto the balls of your feet, hold for 1 second, lower slowly.

Form cue: Use a wall for balance if needed. Slow, controlled reps build more than fast bouncing.
