About THE EMPIRE

A private, invitation-only platform connecting whitelisted users to vetted third-party affiliate campaigns — with transparency and compliance at its core.

Our Story

Why We Built This Platform

THE EMPIRE AFFILIATE MARKETING HUB was founded on a simple observation: the affiliate marketing industry lacked a structured, compliance-first platform that prioritised transparency between campaign networks and the individuals who promote them.

Registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria (RC 9021905), we set out to build a platform that would bridge that gap — not as a marketplace open to everyone, but as a curated, invitation-only system where every participant is verified, every transaction is documented, and every payout flows through licensed payment gateways.

Today, our platform serves whitelisted users who have completed KYC verification. We also offer a growing library of free digital resources to the broader community, because we believe that practical tools should be accessible to everyone.

Closed Affiliate Platform

Our Principles
