### Platform Type

This is a **closed, invitation-only platform**. Access is restricted to whitelisted users who have completed KYC verification. No public onboarding.

---

### Payment Infrastructure (Licensed Payment Gateways)

The platform uses **licensed third-party payment gateways** for API-based earnings reconciliation and disbursement. All transfers are executed by licensed providers — not by Empire.

**Key Features:**
- No deposit acceptance
- No user fund holding
- KYC required before any payout
- Full audit trail
- Idempotency protection

---

### Onboarding & KYC Process

Users requesting access must provide:
- Personal information (name, email, phone)
- Bank account details (bank name, account number, account holder name)
- KYC verification (government ID) before any payout

All information is collected during the access request process.
