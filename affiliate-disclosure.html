THE EMPIRE provides free digital resources — templates, guides, spreadsheets, and toolkits — at absolutely no cost to you. To fund the creation, hosting, and distribution of these resources, we partner with advertising networks who compensate us when users complete offers on our platform.

2. What This Means for You

No extra cost: Completing offers through our links does not cost you anything additional. The advertiser pays us, not you.

Same offers: Whether you access an offer through our site or directly through the advertiser, the offer terms are identical.

Honest recommendations: We only feature offers from established, reputable advertising networks. We do not accept partnerships with companies we wouldn't use ourselves.

Your choice: You are never required to complete any offer. Multiple offer options are provided on each resource page so you can choose the one that works best for you.

We work with established CPA (Cost Per Action) advertising networks, including but not limited to CPAGrip. These networks connect advertisers with publishers like us. All offers displayed on our site are served through these regulated advertising platforms.

· Email submissions: Enter your email address for a chance to win prizes or receive newsletters.
· Mobile app installs: Download and open a free mobile application.

· Free trial sign-ups: Register for a free trial of a service.
· Premium offers: Some higher-value resources may require offers that involve a purchase or paid subscription.

5. FTC Compliance

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials. We believe in transparency and want you to make informed decisions about the offers you choose to complete.

We are committed to providing genuine value through the resources we offer. Our editorial content — including the descriptions, guides, and information on our resource pages — is created independently of our advertising partnerships. Advertisers do not influence the content of our resources.
